# False Positive Rate

False Positive Rate is a sub-metric used in the calculation of the [True Skill Statistic](../true_skill_statistic.md),
the [J Statistic](../j_statistic.md), and [Youden's Index](../youdens_index.md).

It is equal to the following:

$$
\text{False Positive Rate} = \frac{false\_positive}{false\_positive + true\_negative}
$$
